How to install

Because the app comes straight from us rather than the Play Store, your phone will ask for permission once. The exact wording varies a little between phones.

Is this safe?

Yes. Android shows these warnings for any app that doesn't come from the Play Store, not because anything is wrong with this one. Gathered 2026 was built by Hills Baptist Church and simply shows information about the conference: the schedule, electives, speakers, kids and youth details, venue and merch.

  • No sign-in, and it doesn't collect any personal information.
  • It doesn't ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • It can't make purchases. Links like registration and the merch store open in your normal web browser.

  1. Download the app

    Tap Download the app above. If your phone asks whether to keep the file, tap Download or Download anyway.

  2. Open the file

    When it finishes, tap Open in the message that pops up. If you miss it, find gathered-2026.apk in your Downloads or Files app and tap it.

  3. Allow the install

    You'll probably see a message like this:

    For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source.

    Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then tap the back arrow.

  4. Tap Install

    If Google Play Protect shows a warning or offers to scan the app, tap More details then Install anyway, or let it scan and then install.

  5. Open Gathered 2026

    Tap Open. The app is now on your home screen or in your app list, with the terracotta gathered. icon.

Samsung phones

If the install is blocked straight away with a message about Auto Blocker:

  1. Open Settings, then Security and privacy, then Auto Blocker.
  2. Turn Auto Blocker off.
  3. Go back and install the app using the steps above.
  4. Turn Auto Blocker back on afterwards.

Once it's installed

  • You can switch Allow from this source off again in Settings, under Apps, your browser, then Install unknown apps. The app keeps working.
  • Everything is stored on your phone, so the app works even without reception at the venue.
